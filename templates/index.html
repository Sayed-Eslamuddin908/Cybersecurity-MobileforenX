Network Status

⚠ Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi / LAN

Wireless ADB is a local network protocol. It cannot work if:

  • Your phone is on mobile data (4G/5G)
  • Kali and phone are on different Wi-Fi networks
  • The Wi-Fi has AP isolation enabled
  • Either device is connected to a VPN
